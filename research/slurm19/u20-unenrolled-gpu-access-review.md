# u20 unenrolled GPU access

On 2026-10-30 a real, short direct `O_RDWR` open of `/dev/nvidia0` was denied for the enrolled `timzhang` account, but succeeded for `nobody`, which is not in the training group. Both descriptors were immediately closed; no GPU work was launched. The device ACL had `other::rw-`. Thus the live policy does not prevent every ordinary local account from bypassing scheduling. The administrator `u20` and root are intentionally privileged; this is not a claim that privileged users can be constrained by the platform.

The staged ACL script now explicitly sets `other::---` on its existing NVIDIA compute-device allowlist while retaining the training-group denial, the scheduler runtime-group grant and named administrator grants. It has not been deployed. Existing additional named user or group ACLs may still grant access; other device files, credentials, service users, udev ordering, container capabilities and GPU cgroups require separate review. The current desktop processes (Xorg, GNOME, RViz, Sunshine and Edge) were observed under the `u20` administrator identity, but that point-in-time inventory does not prove that every desktop/login service will keep working. Validate with temporary ACL fixtures and a non-disruptive controlled deployment/rollback before changing production devices.
