The helper now removes `other` access and explicitly retains administrator, scheduled runtime group and present `gdm` display-manager access. Udev continues to invoke the same helper path. Slurm, the training backend, their configuration and the quota controller were not replaced or restarted.

The guarded rollout includes automatic restoration of both on a detected failure. Rollback was not required or exercised. Before manual recovery, verify device identity and backup integrity; restore the saved ACL with `setfacl --restore` and restore the old helper, then verify services and authorized access.

After the change, both direct opens were denied; `u20`, `gdm`, and the simulated scheduler runtime identity could open `/dev/nvidia0`. A temporary regular-file ACL fixture also exercised those identities and was removed by its own cleanup.

A 16-by-16 CUDA matrix multiplication completed with exit code 0 and logged `CUDA_PROBE_OK NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 16.0`. This was a short correctness probe on the currently deployed scheduler, not a test of the staged monthly controller.
- Limits: this verifies the tested identities and current device paths only.
